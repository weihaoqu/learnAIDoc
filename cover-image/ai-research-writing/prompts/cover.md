A conceptual cover image for an article about using AI agents in academic research and paper writing workflows.

## Visual Composition

Warm elegant background (soft cream/ivory with subtle gold accents). Center-left: a stylized open notebook or manuscript page with clean text lines. From the right side, multiple layered translucent panels float above the manuscript — each representing a research skill (a magnifying glass for literature search, a network graph for citations, a radar chart for peer review, a target icon for journal matching). The panels are connected by thin golden lines suggesting a workflow pipeline.

At the bottom: a subtle row of academic database logos represented as abstract geometric shapes (circles and hexagons) feeding data upward into the skill panels.

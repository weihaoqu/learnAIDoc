A conceptual cover about cognitive fatigue from overusing AI tools.

## Visual Composition

Warm-to-red gradient background suggesting heat/overload. Center: a stylized brain icon with circuit-like patterns, visibly overheating — smoke wisps or heat waves rising from it. Around the brain, multiple AI tool icons (chat bubbles, code windows, search icons) compete for attention, overlapping and crowding. A subtle "overload" meter or gauge at the bottom showing red/critical level.

## Style Notes

- Flat vector, warm/hot tones (orange, red, amber fading to cool at edges)
- Bold mood — conveys urgency and warning
- The brain should look stressed/overloaded, not broken
- Clean sans-serif "AI Brain Fry" title
- No realistic humans
- 40% whitespace despite the crowding theme

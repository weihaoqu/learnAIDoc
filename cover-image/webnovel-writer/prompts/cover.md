A conceptual cover about AI writing long novels without forgetting context.

## Visual Composition

Warm cream/parchment background. Center: an open book with pages flowing outward, transforming into a network graph on one side (entity graph with character nodes and relationship lines) and stacks of chapter markers on the other side (RAG-retrieved context). A quill pen hovers over the book. Small icons represent characters, plot threads, and narrative elements.

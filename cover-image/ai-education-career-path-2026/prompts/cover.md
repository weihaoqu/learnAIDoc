# Cover Image Prompt

Create a conceptual flat-vector cover image for a student-facing wiki entry about AI+Education career paths.

## Visual Concept

Show a three-layer pathway for students entering AI+Education:

1. **Learning Science** — represented by a small open book + brain icon.
2. **Educational Data** — represented by a clean dashboard panel with dots, bars, and student-event traces.
3. **Generative AI Teaching Tools** — represented by a tool/workflow panel with a small assistant icon and lesson-material cards.

The three layers should connect into one forward path, suggesting that the strongest student profile combines all three rather than choosing only one.

## Color Direction

Use a cool, academic palette: deep navy background, white and pale blue panels, teal/cyan connector lines, and one warm amber accent for the "student opportunity" path. Keep the palette professional and not one-note.

## Composition

- Title text: "AI+Education Career Path" in clean sans-serif, large and readable.
- Main visual: three connected panels or stepping stones across the center.
- Add a small compass or graduation-cap icon near the path to suggest career navigation.
- 45-55% whitespace; polished teaching-diagram feel.
- No realistic humans, no photos, no decorative blobs.
- Stable 16:9 layout suitable for wiki cards.

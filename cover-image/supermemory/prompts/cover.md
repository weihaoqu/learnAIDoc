A conceptual cover about an AI memory engine that makes AI remember users across conversations.

## Visual Composition

Cool blue-purple gradient background. Center: a stylized brain icon made of interconnected nodes/dots, with small fact cards or memory snippets floating around it (like "prefers Python", "lives in NYC", "budget: $5K"). Some older cards are fading out (auto-forget). Arrows flow from a chat bubble into the brain and back out, suggesting memory being stored and retrieved.

## Style Notes

- Flat vector, cool tones (blue, purple, teal)
- Clean sans-serif "Supermemory" title
- The brain/network should feel organized, not chaotic
- Fading cards suggest temporal awareness
- 50% whitespace, calm and intelligent feel
- No realistic humans

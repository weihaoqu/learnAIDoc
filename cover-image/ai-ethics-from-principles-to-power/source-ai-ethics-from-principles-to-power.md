AI Ethics — From Principles to Power Structures

AI ethics should not be taught as a checklist of noble words. This wiki entry uses four survey and review papers to build a teaching map from recurring ethical principles to design concerns, governance commitments, lived realities, and power structures.

Key ideas:
- Principles such as transparency, fairness, non-maleficence, responsibility, and privacy are useful vocabulary, but not implementation.
- Governance turns principles into commitments: disclosure, auditability, ownership, redress, and enforcement.
- Ethical failures can appear inside AI design or in human-AI interaction.
- Lived realities and power structures reveal who benefits, who carries risk, and who can contest the system.

A conceptual cover about an open-source screen recording and editing tool.

## Visual Composition

Vivid gradient background (purple to pink). Center: a stylized screen/monitor icon with a record button (red circle) and a play button, flanked by small icons showing editing features — cursor with zoom rings, scissors/cut, subtitle bars, webcam bubble. Below: export icons for MP4 and GIF formats. Subtle timeline bar at the bottom.

## Style Notes

- Flat vector, vibrant and energetic
- Vivid purples, pinks, with bright accent colors (red for record, green for export)
- Clean sans-serif "Recordly" title
- Feels modern, creator-focused
- No realistic humans
- 40% whitespace

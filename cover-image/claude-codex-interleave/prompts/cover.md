A clean, modern flat-vector conceptual illustration on a cool palette (soft slate blue, teal, and pale cyan over a light off-white background). The concept is a two-model AI review loop.

Visual anchor, offset slightly left: two simplified abstract "review cards" (rounded rectangles) facing each other. The left card is teal and carries a small pen / draft glyph (the author that writes). The right card is deep blue and carries a small magnifier-with-checkmark glyph (the reviewer that gates). Between the two cards, a stylized document or code sheet passes through a subtle vertical "gate" line. A smooth circular loop arrow wraps around both cards to suggest an iterate-until-approved cycle, exiting at a small green checkmark (SHIP). A faint balance-scale motif is integrated into the gate to evoke arbitration.

Composition: 40 to 60 percent whitespace, generous breathing room, balanced negative space on the lower-right for the title. Flat vector style: crisp geometric shapes, minimal soft shadows, no heavy gradients, and absolutely no realistic humans (only simplified abstract shapes and icons).

Title text in a clean modern sans-serif, dark slate, placed in the lower-right area: "Claude Drafts, Codex Gates".

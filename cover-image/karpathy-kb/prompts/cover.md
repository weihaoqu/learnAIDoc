A conceptual cover image about building personal knowledge bases with AI, inspired by Karpathy's approach.

## Visual Composition

Warm cream/amber background. Center: a stylized open book or wiki that transforms into a network graph — pages on the left side, connected nodes on the right side, showing the transition from raw documents to structured, interlinked knowledge. Small document icons (papers, notes, images) flow into the left side. On the right, the nodes glow with warm amber/gold connections.

Above: the title "LLM Knowledge Bases" in clean sans-serif. A subtle brain or lightbulb icon near the title suggesting knowledge/insight.

## Style Notes

- Flat vector, warm and inviting academic feel
- Cream, amber, gold, with deep brown accents
- Clean sans-serif typography
- 50% whitespace, uncluttered

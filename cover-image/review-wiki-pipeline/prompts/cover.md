# Cover Image Prompt

Create a conceptual cover image for a technical article about building an autonomous batch AI quality review pipeline.

## Visual Concept

A flat-vector illustration showing an assembly-line / conveyor belt of document pages flowing through a quality pipeline. The pipeline has three distinct stages visualized as stations:

1. **Input stage (left)** — A stack of document pages entering the conveyor, some with red warning indicators (missing sections, broken links)
2. **Review stage (center)** — A magnifying glass or inspection station with a glowing AI eye, examining each page. Small checkmarks and fix indicators appear around it
3. **Output stage (right)** — Clean, polished documents exiting with green checkmarks, neatly organized

Above the pipeline, a circular progress indicator shows "162/162" to suggest batch completion.

A subtle loop arrow wraps from the output back to the input, representing the `/loop` recurring automation.

## Color Direction

MUST use a **cool palette**: steel blues (#4A90D9), slate grays (#6B7B8D), white backgrounds, with green (#4CAF50) accent for completed items and soft red (#E57373) for flagged items. Clean, technical feel.

## Composition

- **Whitespace**: 50% breathing room
- **Visual anchor**: Pipeline centered horizontally
- **Title**: "Building /review-wiki" in clean sans-serif at top, large and readable
- **No realistic humans** — use abstract geometric shapes for the AI reviewer
- **Style**: Flat vector, minimal gradients, crisp lines, technical diagram aesthetic

## Mood

Balanced — professional and technical, but approachable. Suggests automation, reliability, and systematic quality.

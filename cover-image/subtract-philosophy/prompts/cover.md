A conceptual cover about the philosophy of removing layers rather than adding them when building AI applications.

## Visual Composition

Cool blue-teal gradient background. Center: a minus/subtract symbol prominently displayed, with a cluttered stack of tool icons on the left side (many specialized tools, overlapping, chaotic) flowing toward the right side where only 2-3 clean general-purpose tool icons remain. The transition suggests simplification. Above or beside: a small seedling/plant growing (representing "grown AI") with roots that become the subtract symbol.

## Style Notes

- Flat vector, calm and philosophical
- Cool tones: teal, blue, subtle green for the plant
- Clean sans-serif "Subtract, Don't Add" title
- The left side (cluttered) in muted tones, the right side (clean) in bright cool colors
- No realistic humans
- 50% whitespace, minimalist aesthetic reinforces the message

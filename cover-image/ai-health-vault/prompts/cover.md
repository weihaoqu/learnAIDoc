A conceptual cover image for an article about a private, AI-powered family health management system built on Obsidian.

## Visual Composition

Soft cool-toned background (light blue-grey gradient). Center: a stylized Obsidian vault icon (a rounded rectangle resembling a folder or safe) with a medical cross or heart symbol on it. Inside the vault, faint layered document outlines suggest organized health records.

Around the vault, 4-6 small floating icons representing the key skills: a camera (report extraction), a pill capsule (medication), a trend line chart (analysis), a calendar (follow-ups), a smartwatch (Apple Watch), and a speech bubble (plain language). These icons are connected to the vault by thin lines, suggesting data flowing in.

A small family silhouette (2-3 abstract figures) at the bottom or side, suggesting this is for family use.

## Style Notes

- Flat vector, clean and medical/clinical feel
- Cool blues, teals, and white with subtle green accents (health theme)
- Clean sans-serif "AI Health Vault" title
- Calm, trustworthy, private feel — not clinical/sterile
- No realistic human faces — use abstract silhouettes

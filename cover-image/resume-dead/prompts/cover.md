A bold conceptual cover about traditional resumes being replaced by .claude/ folders as the new developer career card.

## Visual Composition

Dark background (charcoal/navy). Left side: a traditional paper resume icon, faded/crossed out or crumbling apart. Right side: a glowing terminal/code folder icon labeled ".claude/" with structured files inside (CLAUDE.md, skills/, agents/), radiating with vibrant teal/cyan light. An arrow or transition effect flows from the old resume to the new folder.

## Style Notes

- Flat vector, high contrast, bold mood
- Dark background with teal/cyan accents for the .claude/ folder
- The resume should look outdated/faded, the folder should look modern/vibrant
- Clean sans-serif "Resumes Are Dead" title at top
- No realistic humans
- 40% whitespace

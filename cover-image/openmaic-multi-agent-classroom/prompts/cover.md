# Cover Image Prompt

Conceptual flat-vector illustration for an article about OpenMAIC, Tsinghua University's open-source multi-agent AI classroom platform. The image should visually communicate the idea of an AI-driven virtual classroom with multiple agent participants — a teacher and several student agents collaborating around a shared learning space.

## Visual concept

A stylized minimalist classroom scene viewed from a slight three-quarter angle. At the center, a glowing digital whiteboard or screen displays simple geometric knowledge symbols (a node graph, a curve, a small equation glyph). A simplified silhouette teacher-agent stands beside the whiteboard, gesturing toward it. Around the front of the scene, 3-4 simplified silhouette student-agents sit at minimal desks, each with a small thought-bubble or speech-bubble icon to suggest active participation, questions, and debate. Subtle network lines connect all the agents to the central whiteboard, visualizing multi-agent orchestration.

## Style

- Flat-vector illustration, clean geometric shapes, no realism
- Cool palette: deep blues, teals, soft cyans, with one accent color (warm coral or amber) for the central whiteboard glow and the connection lines
- Generous whitespace (40-60%), composition centered with the whiteboard as the visual anchor
- Simplified silhouette characters — NO realistic faces or detailed features
- Clean sans-serif typography for the title

## Title

Place the title **"OpenMAIC: Multi-Agent AI Classroom"** in the upper-left or upper area in clean sans-serif. Keep it readable but not dominant.

## Mood

Balanced, modern, intellectually inviting — evokes structured learning, collaboration, and AI-augmented education. Avoid anything chaotic or overly sci-fi.

## Aspect ratio

16:9 widescreen.

A conceptual cover for a practical guide about building a personal knowledge base system.

## Visual Composition

Light blue-grey gradient background. Center: a stylized workspace showing three connected layers — a folder icon (raw sources) on the left flowing into a structured wiki/book icon (compiled knowledge) in the center, flowing into a brain/lightbulb icon (personal understanding) on the right. Arrows connect the three stages. Below the main flow, four small tool icons represent the four operations: a funnel (ingest), a magnifying glass (query), a wrench (lint), and a microscope (research).

## Style Notes

- Flat vector, clean educational infographic style
- Cool blues, teals, with white and light grey
- Clean sans-serif title "Building a Personal Knowledge Base"
- Calm, organized, approachable feel
- No realistic humans
- 50% whitespace

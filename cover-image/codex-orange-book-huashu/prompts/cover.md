# Cover prompt — Codex Orange Book

**Title (display exactly):** Codex Orange Book

## Concept
Flat-vector cover for a wiki entry on 花叔's "Codex Orange Book" — a bilingual (Chinese + English) practitioner book on OpenAI Codex. Visual idea: a stylized **book** rendered as a flat-vector illustration in **warm orange tones** (honoring the "orange book" naming), with subtle indicators of its bilingual nature and its coverage of the "five Codex forms."

## Visual elements
- **Center:** a stylized flat-vector hardcover book, slightly angled (3/4 view), with an orange cover and visible spine. The cover features a simple geometric mark — three or four small horizontal lines (representing chapters), plus a subtle "{}" code-brackets glyph in the upper-right corner.
- **Around the book:** five small flat-icon glyphs in a gentle arc above the book, representing the five Codex deployment forms — a terminal-prompt `>`, a small monitor, a cloud, a chevron-brackets `</>`, and a small browser-tab glyph. Each is a simple line-icon in a muted blue-grey to contrast with the orange book.
- **A small `中 / EN` badge** in one corner of the book cover, indicating bilingual.
- **Background:** uncluttered warm cream/ivory gradient with very faint dot grid (10% opacity). Subtle vignette at corners for warmth.
- **Mood:** approachable, scholarly, practical. NOT corporate, NOT chaotic.

## Color palette (warm — orange anchor)
- Book cover: warm terracotta orange (#D87A52 / #C97B5A)
- Book spine darker: deep terracotta (#A65A38)
- Five form-glyphs: muted steel blue (#5B89A6) at moderate weight
- Background: cream/ivory (#FAF6EE → #F2EDE0)
- Text: deep navy (#1F3445)
- Subtle gold/honey accent on the "{}" glyph on the book cover

## Typography
- Title text — render exactly as: `Codex Orange Book`
- Clean modern sans-serif (Inter / Söhne), lower-center, balanced weight
- Small "中 / EN" badge legible only at projector distance
- No subtitle, no tagline

## Composition
- 55% whitespace
- Book centered, slightly large
- Five form-glyphs in a gentle upper arc above the book
- Title bottom band, lower-center
- 16:9

## Avoid
- Realistic photos
- Specific brand logos (OpenAI, GitHub, Codex wordmark)
- Cluttered "infographic" feel
- Multiple competing accent colors
- Aggressive imagery
- Cool palette — this is warm/orange

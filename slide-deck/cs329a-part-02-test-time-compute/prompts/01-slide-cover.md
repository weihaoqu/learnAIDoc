## Teaching objective
State why extra inference compute is not itself a reliability guarantee.

## Speaker notes
More attempts can expose a good candidate that one-shot decoding misses. The deployed system must still allocate its budget and identify success. Ask Q: If one of 100 candidates is correct but a wrong answer is returned, what improved and what did not?

## Speaker notes
Executable tests can cheaply reject candidates that violate specified checks; they do not prove general correctness. Proof assistants can check formalized proofs. Writing, policy, and science depend on incomplete evaluators or expensive experts. Learned selectors can plateau even while coverage rises.

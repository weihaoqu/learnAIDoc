## Teaching objective
Explain why a benchmark average can look power-law scaled.

## Speaker notes
Easy problems leave the failure pool quickly. A long tail of tasks with tiny single-attempt success probabilities dominates what remains. The power-law observation is aggregate, not a claim that each problem follows a power law.

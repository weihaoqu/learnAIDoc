## Teaching objective

## Speaker notes
Generation and reliability are different system properties. A model may contain useful capability but expose it inconsistently. An agent needs a loop that tests actions, observes consequences, and recovers. Ask Q: What part of the loop turns a plausible answer into a trustworthy action?

## Teaching objective
Explain the role of fine-tuning, instruction tuning, and preference optimization.

## Speaker notes
Pretraining develops broad capability. Fine-tuning sharpens it on selected data. Instruction tuning teaches request-response patterns. Preference optimization rewards selected evaluator criteria. A preference model is not automatically a truth detector.

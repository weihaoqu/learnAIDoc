## Teaching objective
Distinguish a chatbot response from an agent task loop.

## Speaker notes
A chatbot mainly returns information. An agent manages task state, acts through tools, observes consequences, and decides whether to continue, stop, or escalate. Autonomy can still be bounded by approval gates.

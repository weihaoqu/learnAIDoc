# Teaching Guide

## Big picture

This lesson should feel like an investigation, not a prompt demonstration. Students first see why "revise until 95" is attractive. They then test whether the number corresponds to observable improvement and redesign the workflow around evidence.

## Instructor preparation

Provide these before class:

- one bounded task
- one starter source packet
- one explicit rubric
- one deliberately imperfect starter artifact
- one verification checklist
- one logging template
- access to the same model and tools for all teams where possible

## Recommended task

Ask teams to convert a short lecture excerpt into three teaching slides. Seed the starter artifact with one unsupported claim, one missing requirement, and one visible layout defect. This makes verification differences observable within 22 minutes.

## Three conditions

### A. Single pass

Students generate once and submit. This is the cost and quality baseline.

### B. Self-score to 95

Students use the viral pattern without external checks. They record every self-score and revision.

### C. Evidence-verified loop

Students apply the contract, source map, rendering checks, targeted revision, two-cycle limit, and human handoff.

## Debrief questions

1. Did the numerical self-score track the instructor rubric?
2. Which defects could self-critique find without tools?
3. Which defects required source or render evidence?
4. Did any revision introduce a new error?
5. When did another iteration stop being worth its cost?
6. Which final decisions still required a human?

## Assessment stance

Do not reward Condition C automatically. Reward accurate observation, good verifier design, explicit limitations, and honest reporting. A student who concludes that the simple baseline was sufficient can earn full credit if the evidence supports that conclusion.

## Accessibility notes

Read the labels and relationships in each diagram aloud rather than relying on color. Provide the PDF or PPTX in advance. Allow students to use the tabular workflow and logging template instead of interpreting the visual diagrams alone.

- 13 slides
- 16:9, 1600x900
- 60-minute class
- Independent teaching companion

## Narrative arc

1. Start with the appealing prompt students may already encounter.
2. Replace a vague score with an engineering model of feedback.
3. Establish the prompt-agent distinction.
4. Ground the pattern in primary research and its limitations.
5. Decide when the approach should be used.
6. Build the complete loop and verifier hierarchy.
7. Add stopping and authority.
8. Apply the framework across situations.
9. Walk through a concrete slide-production case.
10. Let students compare three workflows rather than accept the instructor's claim.
11. Grade evidence and judgment rather than surface polish.

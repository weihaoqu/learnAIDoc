# Slide Deck Analysis

Topic:
AI ethics in college assignment policy.

Audience:
- College teachers designing AI-use policies.
- Students learning ethical AI use.
- Education leaders who need policy language that is practical rather than merely aspirational.

Content signals:
- AI ethics survey papers.
- Higher-education policy.
- Student assignments, disclosure, grading, privacy, fairness, accountability.
- Classroom discussion and homework design.

Recommended style:
- Academic seminar plus policy briefing.
- Light editorial canvas, strong black typography, restrained color accents, diagrams and decision tables.
- Avoid stock AI visuals. The subject is governance and learning, so the visual language should be classrooms, documents, rules, boundaries, and accountability flows.

Recommended language:
- English.
- Reason: the wiki is in English and the paper references are English. Q may later adapt the post into Chinese social content, but the knowledge-base artifact should remain citation precise.

Recommended slide count:
- 14 slides.
- Reason: enough space for the opening scenario, four-paper map, policy model, assignment rubric, and student homework without turning each slide into a wall of text.

Design notes:
- Use one main sentence headline per slide.
- Prefer tables, cards, and arrows over paragraphs.
- Keep the four papers visibly distinct from Q's classroom-policy synthesis.
- Make the ending actionable: allow, disclose, protect, assess.

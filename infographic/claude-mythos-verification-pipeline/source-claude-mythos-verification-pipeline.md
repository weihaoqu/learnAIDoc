# Claim Pipeline for AI Security Announcements

Source section from the LearnAI wiki entry "Claude Mythos and the Verification Problem in AI Security Claims".

```text
Claim pipeline for AI security announcements

  Vendor headline
       |
       v
  Technical report
       |
       v
  Reproducible artifacts
       |
       v
  Independent validation
       |
       v
  Procurement / policy / governance decision

Verification crisis = a later decision is much stronger than the evidence
                      that survived the earlier steps.
```

Purpose: turn the ASCII claim pipeline into a visual process infographic for a LearnAI wiki post. The infographic should teach that AI security claims should move from headline to evidence to independent validation before they drive governance or procurement decisions.

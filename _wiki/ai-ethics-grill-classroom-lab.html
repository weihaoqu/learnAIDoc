0-8 minutes

Four lenses, one difficult case

Use these ideas as decision tools. They are a classroom synthesis, not a claim that the four papers offer one unified college policy.

Jobin

Shared words are not shared rules

Transparency, fairness, privacy, responsibility, and safety recur across guidelines, but their meanings and implementation differ.

Ask: What exactly does this principle require here?
Corrêa

Principles need a delivery system

Repeated commitments do not become governance until duties, procedures, implementation, and enforcement are specified.

Ask: Who must do what, using which process?
Giarmoleo

Rules and judgment work together

Act-centered responses govern actions through rules and procedures. Agent-centered responses develop the judgment to handle cases rules miss.

Ask: What rule and what human capability are both needed?
Groen

Look beyond principles

Ethical analysis also examines lived realities and power: access, monitoring, vulnerability, discretion, and who bears the consequences.

Ask: Who gains power, and who absorbs the risk?
Open paper details and source cautions

Jobin, Ienca, and Vayena (2019). The global landscape of AI ethics guidelines. Review of 84 guideline documents.

Corrêa et al. (2023). Worldwide AI Ethics. Review of 200 guidelines and governance recommendations.

Giarmoleo et al. (2024). What ethics can say on artificial intelligence. Systematic literature review.

Groen, Sharon, and Becker (2026). An overview of AI ethics. Narrative overview through principles, lived realities, and power structures.

Claude is not one of these sources. Verify any paper claim it produces against the linked paper or the supplied source summaries.

Anchor case

The polished report

A professor assigns an analytical report. The syllabus says students may "use AI responsibly" and must disclose assistance, but neither term is defined.

A student submits a polished report and writes only, "AI helped with this assignment." During a short discussion, the student cannot explain or verify two important claims.

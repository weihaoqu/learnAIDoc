Anthropic launched **Anthropic Academy** in March 2026 — a free, self-paced learning platform with 13 courses covering everything from basic Claude usage to production API development and MCP server building. All courses are free, require only an email signup, and award official certificates on completion.

*Source: [Anthropic Academy](https://anthropic.skilljar.com/) | [Awesome Agents: 13 Free Courses Overview](https://awesomeagents.ai/news/anthropic-academy-free-ai-curriculum/) | [Class Central: Anthropic Academy](https://www.classcentral.com/provider/anthropic-academy)*

## Complete Course Catalog

## Academic Partnerships

The AI Fluency track was co-developed with:
- **Professor Joseph Feller** — University College Cork
- **Professor Rick Dakan** — Ringling College of Art and Design

The Creative Commons license means any university or institution can adopt and modify these courses for their own curriculum — no licensing fees, no approval needed.

## Beyond Academy: The Certified Architect Exam

For those who complete the developer track, Anthropic also offers the **Claude Certified Architect — Foundations (CCA-F)** exam: 60 questions, 120 minutes, proctored, scenario-based. It tests production-grade Claude application design at enterprise scale. Free prep materials are available through the Academy.

## How to Get Started

1. Go to [anthropic.skilljar.com](https://anthropic.skilljar.com/)
2. Sign up with your email (no credit card)
3. Start with **Claude 101** if you're new, or jump to your track
4. Complete courses at your own pace
5. Earn certificates on completion
